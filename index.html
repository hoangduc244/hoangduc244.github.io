Autonomous Vacuum Robot with ROS 2 SLAM

Overview: Built a compact autonomous vacuum robot using ROS 2, Raspberry Pi, Arduino Mega, and RPLIDAR for LiDAR-based indoor mapping and robot localization.

My Role: Integrated the ROS 2 system, Arduino motor bridge, encoder odometry, LiDAR driver, TF coordinate frames, Docker environment, and browser-based robot dashboard.

Tools: ROS 2 Jazzy, Raspberry Pi, Arduino, RPLIDAR, Docker, Python, C++, JavaScript, rosbridge, GitHub

Result: Created a working SLAM system that builds a 2D occupancy map, displays live LiDAR scans, tracks robot pose, and supports web-based teleoperation using W/A/S/D controls.

Rover Robot

Overview: Built a rover robot using an Arduino to control two encoder DC motors, a stepper motor for vertical movement, and a servo motor for the gripper.

My Role: Programmed the motor control, vertical lift movement, and gripper actuation using C++.

Tools: Arduino, C++, Encoder DC Motors, Stepper Motor, Servo Motor, LiDAR, Camera, IMU, GPS

Result: Developed a rover platform capable of controlled driving, vertical lifting, and gripping objects with sensor integration.

Payload Delivery Mechanism

Mission: The objective of this project was to develop a drone-based delivery system capable of transporting emergency supplies, such as water bottles and flashlights, to people stranded in disaster or emergency situations. The goal was to provide critical resources to individuals who needed assistance before rescue teams could reach them.

My Role: As the Delivery Subteam Lead, I led the design and prototyping of the payload delivery mechanism. I coordinated with team members, evaluated multiple release concepts, and guided the integration of the payload system with the aircraft platform.

Design: The mechanism was required to securely hold the payload during flight while ensuring a reliable and repeatable release at the designated drop location. The design process involved CAD modeling, 3D printing, mechanical testing, and iterative improvements based on flight-test results.

Result: Successfully demonstrated payload deployment during flight testing and provided a lightweight, reliable solution for aerial delivery missions.

Autonomous Line-Following Car

Overview: Developed an Arduino-based autonomous vehicle capable of following a complex black line track containing jumps, sharp curves, and S-shaped sections.

My Role: Designed and implemented the PID control algorithm used to continuously correct steering error and maintain accurate tracking along the course.

Tools: Arduino, C++, PID Control, Infrared Sensors, DC Motors, Motor Drivers

Result: Successfully navigated complex track layouts with improved stability, reduced oscillation, and accurate line tracking through curves and transitions.
